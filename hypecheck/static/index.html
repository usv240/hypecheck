HypeCheck Checks a draft health press release against the paper it reports, flags every claim that says more than the study found, has Claude rewrite it, and re-checks the rewrite before anything is published.

Threshold is calibrated, not hand-picked: at most 10% of faithful sentences are flagged (measured 7.7% on 441 held-out expert-labelled pairs).

Results appear here. Red = claims more than the paper. Green = within what the paper supports.

Built for the AWS Agent Experience Hackathon. Decision model: Strands Decider 2B, pattern #guardrails. Generative model: Claude on Amazon Bedrock. Evidence: Sumner et al. 2014 (BMJ), Adams et al. 2019 (BMC Medicine, RCT).

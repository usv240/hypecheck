## What the lists show

- **7 of the 75 dangerous misses sit in the review zone** (score between 0.3986 and 0.5001), so an editor sees them; the other 68 are silent.
- **The commonest dangerous miss is a rung disagreement on the release:** Decider read it as "linked" where the experts read "may cause" (22 of 75). Headlines with no verb ("Scientists identify gene responsible for…") are the hard case.
- **Needless blocks cluster where the paper's own conclusion is hedged** ("may") and the release states the finding plainly. Experts sometimes accept that as faithful; the gate does not.
